RESOLVEAI PRODUCT WARRANTY POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Revised: October 2025

1. STANDARD 1-YEAR LIMITED WARRANTY
- All consumer electronics, smart gadgets, home appliances, and audio gear purchased through ResolveAI include an automatic 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty starting from the date of delivery.
- Coverage includes: Manufacturing defects, premature mechanical failure, firmware faults, and internal component malfunction under normal use.

2. WHAT IS NOT COVERED
- Accidental damage including water submersion, screen cracks from physical drops, or power surge damage.
- Unauthorized third-party repairs, modifications, or tampering with internal tamper-evident seals.
- Cosmetic scratches, normal wear-and-tear, or lost/stolen items.

3. EXTENDED PROTECTION (RESOLVECARE+)
- Customers enrolled in ResolveCare+ enjoy 24-month accidental damage coverage (drops & spills), express replacement units, and zero shipping deductible.

4. HOW TO FILE A WARRANTY CLAIM
Step 1: Contact ResolveAI Chat or Complaint Center with your Order ID (e.g., ORD-1004).
Step 2: Describe the defect and provide a photo or short video showing the issue.
Step 3: If deemed eligible by our warranty diagnostic rules, a warranty replacement or authorized repair ticket is generated within 24 hours.
Step 4: Replacement units are dispatched via express shipping.
