RESOLVEAI E-COMMERCE SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Revised: October 2025

1. SHIPPING TIERS & TIMELINES
- Standard Shipping: 3 to 5 business days across continental zones. Free on orders over $49.
- Expedited / Express Shipping: 1 to 2 business days ($12.99 fee).
- Same-Day Delivery: Available in selected metropolitan hubs for orders placed before 11:00 AM local time ($19.99 fee).
- International Standard Shipping: 7 to 14 business days, subject to local customs clearance.

2. TRACKING INFORMATION
- Tracking numbers are issued as soon as fulfillment assigns the carrier label (within 24 hours of checkout).
- Active carrier tracking links (FedEx, UPS, DHL, OnTrac) show checkpoints: Order Placed -> Fulfillment Center -> In Transit -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered.

3. DELAY NOTIFICATIONS & RESOLUTION
- When severe weather, customs delays, or routing bottlenecks cause a delivery to slip beyond its estimated delivery date, ResolveAI flags the order status as "Delayed".
- Minor delays (1-3 days): Customers receive automated priority tracking updates.
- Extended delays (4-7 days): Customers are offered a $15 inconvenience credit or priority courier re-dispatch.
- Critical delays (>14 days past ETA): Eligible for immediate full refund upon customer request.

4. ADDRESS CHANGES & CANCELLATIONS
- Shipping address modifications can be processed directly via customer service only while the order is in "Processing" status.
- Once an order transitions to "Shipped" or "In Transit", address re-routing requests are handled with carrier dispatch and may incur a $10 carrier re-routing fee.
